## 0. Executive frame: phase is the one representational axis Prizma does not have — and the one the brain demonstrably uses for memory

### 0.1 What the brain does with rhythm (the defensible core)

The empirical case, strongest first:

1. **Theta phase precession** — as a rat traverses a place field, the place cell fires at successively
   earlier theta phases (O'Keefe & Recce 1993 *(knowledge)*; Skaggs et al. 1996 *(knowledge)*).
   Consequence: **position in a trajectory is encoded as phase within a global oscillation**, so a
   short history is *compressed into the phase dimension* and can be read out one item per phase
   subcycle (Jensen & Lisman 1996; Lisman & Idiart 1995's 7±2 gamma-subcycle account of working
   memory span *(knowledge)*). Human iEEG shows analogous precession (Jacobs et al. 2007 *(knowledge)*).
2. **Theta-gamma coupling / multiplexing** — fast gamma activity is amplitude- and phase-modulated by
   a slow theta carrier (Canolty et al. 2006, Science *(knowledge)*); the theta-gamma code proposes
   gamma-rate items ride theta-phase slots (Lisman & Jensen 2013, Neuron *(knowledge)*). Rate and
   phase carry partly independent information (Huxter, Burgess & O'Keefe 2003; Lengyel et al. 2005
   *(knowledge)*) — phase is a *second channel*, not decoration.
3. **Communication through coherence (CTC)** — neuronal groups communicate when their rhythms are
   phase-coherent; incoherent input lands in the suppressed phase of the postsynaptic cycle and is
   gated out (Fries 2005, 2015 *(knowledge)*); interareal phase-locking predicts influence
   (Womelsdorf et al. 2007 *(knowledge)*). Alpha's pulsed inhibition is the inhibitory version of the
   same gate (Jensen & Mazaheri 2010 *(knowledge)*).
4. **Binding by synchrony** — features of the same object fire in synchrony and bind into one
   assembly representation, solving the superposition catastrophe that concatenation/averaging cannot
   (von der Malsburg 1981; Singer & Gray 1995 *(knowledge)*). **This one is contested** — Shadlen &
   Movshon 1999 argued synchrony cannot carry the binding load *(knowledge)* — and this report keeps
   that adversarial citation attached to every synchrony proposal.
5. **Two-stage consolidation on a global rhythm** — theta during exploration *writes*; sharp-wave
   ripples during quiescence/sleep *consolidate* (Buzsáki 1989 two-stage model; Wilson & McNaughton
   1994 replay *(knowledge)*); slow-oscillation/spindle/ripple nesting organizes the handoff across
   scales (Helfrich et al. 2018; Rasch & Born 2013 *(knowledge)*). Cortex is a hierarchy of
   timescales from synapse to hemisphere (Hasson et al. 2008; Kiebel, Daunizeau & Friston 2008
   *(knowledge)*) — directly resonant with Prizma's free-energy identity.

**Honesty note:** the brain's rhythms are *endogenous* — they are generated by the tissue, not stamped
by an external clock. An artificial oscillator that advances one tick per token is in danger of being
"just position indexing wearing a lab coat." The proposals below are written so that each phase
variable is either (a) **data-dependent** (frequency/gain is a function of the current token — the
model controls its own clock, which is the closest legitimate analog of endogeneity available in this
training regime) or (b) demonstrably more than indexing via the pre-registered null (P9). Where the
only honest description is "a clock," the proposal says so.

### 0.2 What the Transformer has and has not (the competitive core)

- **The Transformer already has a phase code — a frozen one.** RoPE is a fixed-frequency rotation of
  q/k; all its "phase" is *absolute positional clocking* (Su et al. 2021 *(knowledge)*). Its failures
  are exactly the failures of a *non-dynamic* phase code: length extrapolation breaks because phase
  frequencies were calibrated at train length; no mechanism lets content modulate the clock.
- **What no member of the delta-rule family (GDN, Mamba-2, GLA, RWKV-7, Prizma today) has:** phase
  dynamics *inside the carried state*. These models carry (content, precision/decay) — Mamba-2's S4
  lineage even carries decaying oscillators in its diagonal modes (S4D; Gu et al. 2022 *(knowledge)*),
  but the delta-rule family that Prizma belongs to carries **real, monotone, non-oscillating** decay:
  in `seq/prizma_seq.py` the gated `alpha` is clamped to [0.5, 1] — every head can only *forget
  monotonically*; half-lives span ~1 token (α=0.5) to hundreds of tokens (α≈0.999), but the phase of a
  memory trace never advances. Old information fades in **amplitude** and carries **zero age tag**
  beyond what amplitude confounds.
- **The pitch that makes phase an inevitability argument, not decoration:** attention's readout scores
  a query against items carrying (content, position). A phase-capable delta state would carry
  **(content, precision, phase)** — a third axis that attention cannot have at O(1) state, because its
  phase lives in a growing KV cache, not in dynamics. If phase-in-state buys length extrapolation or
  capacity at matched params, that is a capability *structurally unavailable* to both the Transformer
  and to today's Prizma. That is the "inevitable rival" story this lens contributes: **own the phase
  axis of the delta-rule family before anyone else notices it is empty.**

### 0.3 The kernel constraint — a feasibility grammar, stated once

The repo's binding constraint is the WY/UT chunk-parallel kernel (`seq/delta.py`). Reading that file
yields a clean four-tier grammar for any phase mechanism; every proposal below is tagged with its tier:

- **T0 — pointwise.** The mechanism touches only per-token features (q, k, v, gates) before the
  kernel. Exactness and speed untouched. Zero risk to the `step()==forward()` guard.
- **T1 — chunk-constant structure.** The mechanism is constant within a chunk (e.g., one rotation
  matrix per head per chunk). Pulled out of the chunk recursion by conjugation; the WY/UT solve is
  unchanged; cost is one small matrix-multiply per chunk boundary (T/C per sequence — negligible).
- **T2 — per-token structured, state-independent.** The mechanism varies per token but never reads the
  running state. The within-chunk recurrence stays an exact unit-lower-triangular solve with modified
  entries. Precedent: the `eta` per-channel lever already does exactly this with batched per-channel
  triangular solves (`seq/delta.py` docstring). T2 costs kernel derivation + some inner-product factor,
  not a sequential scan.
- **T3 — state-dependent gates.** Anything whose gate reads S_{t-1} (the existing `surprise_gate`
  precedent) forces the exact sequential scan — the disclosed ~5×-slower path. **Default: forbidden**
  for phase mechanisms; allowed only as a labeled slow arm to test whether the fast approximation
  left anything on the table.

A design rule follows, and it is also the *scientific* rule: **phase may be a function of the clock or
of the current token, never of the running state.** An open-loop oscillator is also chunk-parallel by
construction; a state-dependent oscillator is both slow and (today) unparallelizable.

## 1. The proposals (ranked by expected value; scores at the end of each)

### P1 — Rotating-frame state: replace monotone forgetting with data-dependent per-head oscillation ("memory traces precess")

**[B→N]** borrowed: oscillatory decay modes (S4D poles *(knowledge)*; Tsodyks et al. 1996 precession
model *(knowledge)*); new: transplanted into the **delta-rule** family's state as a data-dependent,
head-level clock, which no GDN/Mamba-2/GLA/RWKV variant has.

- **WHAT.** Give each head a complex-decay generalization: instead of `S ← α·S + u k^T` with
  α ∈ [0.5, 1], use `S ← ρ·R(ω)·S + u k^T`, where R(ω) is a rotation (in a real 2×2 block or
  complex-pair form per value channel) and ω is produced by a small data-dependent phase head
  (`ω = f_ω(x_t)`, one angle per head — 1 new scalar gate head per block, same param discipline as the
  existing `W_alpha`). Amplitude ρ takes today's α's job; phase ω makes the trace *precess*: the age
  of a written item becomes partially readable from its rotation state, and the readout
  `o = S q` inherits an age-dependent phase the output projection can exploit.
- **WHY (brain).** Theta phase precession (O'Keefe & Recce 1993; Skaggs et al. 1996; Jensen & Lisman
  1996 *(knowledge)*): position/age is encoded as phase of a carrier; downstream readers exploit it.
  This is the single most defensible rhythm claim in the literature, and it maps one-to-one onto "a
  sequence position acts through its phase in the state."
- **WHY (beat transformers).** Targets the repo's two weakest bars. (a) **Length extrapolation**
  (absolute accuracy only ~0.40 at 8× train length): monotone amplitude decay deletes old keys; phase
  preserves magnitude and encodes age orthogonally to strength — a recall readout can in principle be
  *age-selective* rather than strength-limited, which is the same reason RoPE helps attention
  extrapolate relatively. (b) **Capacity**: today all superposed items interfere in-phase; rotated
  writes decorrelate the crosstalk term (see P3 for the strong version; P1 already spreads
  interference across phases). Stability is *free*: R is an isometry, so the repo's Jacobian-
  contractiveness bound (‖J‖ ≤ α; `docs/quad2_theoretical_convergence.md`) carries over verbatim with
  ρ in place of α.
- **HOW (tiered, per §0.3).** **T1 first:** one angle per head per *chunk* (pooled input statistic or
  chunk-clock) → conjugation pulls R out of the WY/UT recursion; exact, kernel untouched, one extra
  rotation per chunk boundary; `step()` applies one rotation per token at inference (O(d_h²) per
  chunk-conjugate amortized — flag: at inference the rotation must be applied per token to S, which is
  d_h × d_h work per head — at d_h=32 this is trivial but must appear in the latency ledger honestly).
  **T2 second:** per-token ω via the generalized inner-product trick in P3's HOW; new kernel
  derivation, precedent exists in the `eta` batched solves. **T3 (state-dependent ω) prohibited by
  default.** Ablations: `phase: off | chunk-clock | chunk-data | token-data`, with `off == identical`
  byte-guard per repo culture; ρ=1 (pure rotation, no decay) as a diagnostic arm.
- **FALSIFIABLE TEST (pre-register before any run).** On `gpu_lengen.py` protocol at matched params:
  Prizma+P1(T1) absolute accuracy at 8× train length **≥ 0.55** (vs ~0.40 today) AND all §4
  diagnostics (MQAR/induction/selective-copy) non-inferior within seed noise. Additionally the
  *null-detection* arm (P9's spectra): trained P1 heads should show peaked per-head rotation-speed
  histograms with data-dependent modulation (ω correlates with token statistics); if trained ω
  collapses to a constant or to 0, the mechanism is inert and the claim dies. 3 seeds minimum, 5 for
  the headline cell, per the B4 lesson.
- **RISKS.** (i) Recall needs a demodulation pathway the model must learn — the query cannot know a
  priori the age of what it seeks; P3's harmonic variant is the principled fix, and P1-T1 may show
  nothing without it. (ii) Rotation interacts with the `quad2` phi map (rotating before vs after φ is
  a real design fork; both arms must be run, one flagged primary *before* results). (iii) Inference
  latency: per-token rotation of S at `step()` is honest O(d_h²) extra — small at d_h=32 but it must
  appear in `gpu_latency.py`, not be waved away. (iv) The endogeneity criticism: a chunk-clock ω is a
  clock, not a brain rhythm; the data-dependent arm is the defensible one and must be the headline arm.
- **IMPACT 8 / FEASIBILITY 6** (T1 arm cheap; T2 kernel derivation is the real cost; T3 banned).

### P2 — Theta/gamma dual-population heads: fast heads write per token, slow heads absorb per chunk (write-then-consolidate inside every chunk)

**[B→N]** borrowed: theta-gamma multiplexing (Lisman & Jensen 2013; Canolty et al. 2006
*(knowledge)*); Buzsáki 1989 encode/consolidate split *(knowledge)*. New: as a *head-population
partition* of a delta-rule mixer with a fixed, chunk-locked transfer rhythm.

- **WHAT.** Partition the H heads into γ-population (fast: today's behavior — per-token gated delta
  writes) and θ-population (slow: receives **one write per chunk**, at the chunk boundary, from a
  learned salience-pooled summary of the chunk — e.g., the γ-state's own read at a fixed query, or a
  learned pooled token). θ-heads are ordinary DeltaNet heads whose *effective* sequence is T/C long
  with β≈β_cap at each write: few, high-precision, long-half-life writes — which is exactly what the
  existing machinery can express with zero new kernel code (a θ head is the same head type run on a
  strided stream; T0/T1 by construction). The γ→θ transfer at each boundary is the artificial analog
  of gamma items riding theta phases and of the encode→consolidate handoff.
- **WHY (brain).** Two-stage memory theory in miniature: fast buffers, slow consolidation, nested
  rhythms (Buzsáki 1989; Helfrich et al. 2018 *(knowledge)*). The window head (w=16 ≈ one gamma
  cycle; chunk=64 ≈ one theta cycle at the loose 4–7γ:1θ ratio *(knowledge)*) already sits in the
  architecture — this proposal makes the *state* heads match the same nesting instead of only the
  conv/window path.
- **WHY (beat transformers).** (a) Targets **B4 char-LM** — the one failed corpus (tiny-shakespeare,
  −0.09) is exactly where long-range structure with rare high-precision writes should help; a θ/γ
  split is a *hypothesis about that specific failure*. (b) Induction heads over long ranges: θ-population
  carries the "library" while γ-population handles the local clause. (c) FLOP story: θ-heads do T/C
  delta writes instead of T — if γ-population can be narrowed (e.g., 5:1 θ:γ instead of today's
  uniform heads), total delta-state FLOPs drop with *more* long-range capacity — the only lever in
  this report that aims at the disclosed 2.14× FLOP deficit and the O(T·d_h²) train cost at once.
- **HOW.** Config knob: `head_timescales: uniform | theta_gamma`, `theta_ratio` (fraction of heads),
  `theta_write: chunk_end_pooled | learned_token`. Everything is existing-head-type + striding: no
  kernel change (T0/T1). Must pass the `off == identical` guard (`theta_ratio=0` ≡ today). Latency and
  memory ledgers re-run; the θ-head strided stream must go through `step()` unchanged (it already is
  O(1) — a θ head simply updates every C-th token; flag: `step()` must carry θ-head state untouched
  between boundary tokens — trivial but new state bookkeeping).
- **FALSIFIABLE TEST.** Pre-register a *dissociation*, not a win-everywhere: on char-LM text8 +
  tiny-shakespeare (the honest B4 re-run, ≥5 seeds on the closest leg), θ/γ split **beats uniform heads
  on tiny-shakespeare specifically** (the corpus whose failure motivated it), with MQAR parity;
  plus the causal probe: ablating θ-heads at eval must degrade long-range induction while sparing
  selective-copy-local, and ablating γ-heads the reverse. If both populations behave identically in
  ablation, the multiplexing story is a naming convention and dies.
- **RISKS.** (i) Reinventing "chunking under a neuroscience name" — the defense is the dissociation
  test: uniform-chunk heads (all heads strided, no split) must be run as the control that isolates the
  *split*, not the stride. (ii) Fixed rhythm is exogenous; the defensible version lets a learned gate
  *skip* θ-writes when the chunk was low-salience (state-independent gate → still T1). (iii) Fewer
  γ-heads may cost MQAR at 130K params — the param-efficiency headline must be re-run, not assumed.
- **IMPACT 8 / FEASIBILITY 7.**

### P3 — Harmonic phase multiplexing of writes: key/value rotations give every memory an age tag and decorrelate crosstalk (the capacity lever)

**[B→N]** borrowed: multiplexed phase/rate coding (Huxter 2003; Lengyel et al. 2005 *(knowledge)*);
fractional-power / phase-encoded bindings in vector-symbol architectures (Plate's HRR; Kanerva 2009
HDC; Frady & Sommer *(knowledge)*); RoPE's relative-phase property (Su et al. 2021 *(knowledge)*).
New: rotations applied **inside the state's write/read algebra** (RoPE-in-the-state), so phase becomes
a property of *remembered* items, not just of compared positions.

- **WHAT.** Rotate k and v by the same per-token (or per-chunk-constant, T1 fallback) rotation: write
  `u (R_t k)^T` into a state that receives `R_t` on the value side too (concretely: rotate v before
  the write, and rotate q at read). Algebra (state-dependent only on the clock, hence T2-eligible):
  an item written at t′ read at t appears rotated by R(t−t′) — **relative phase = age difference**, the
  exact property that makes RoPE extrapolate but here attached to *state contents*, which attention
  cannot do at O(1). Because readout then carries an age-phase, a small set of harmonic readout
  features (correlating the read against a few candidate phase offsets — the "N-step readout" of
  Jensen & Lisman 1996 *(knowledge)*) lets the model attend over *ages within the state* — a second
  retrieval axis at zero state cost.
- **WHY (brain).** Phase precession as memory compression + phase-based readout of a stored sequence
  (O'Keefe & Recce 1993; Jensen & Lisman 1996; Lisman & Idiart 1995 *(knowledge)*); multiplexing two
  codes on one channel (Huxter 2003 *(knowledge)*).
- **WHY (beat transformers).** This is the **capacity** proposal. The repo's own Gershgorin analysis
  (`docs/quad2_theoretical_convergence.md`) bounds MQAR capacity by key-crosstalk (N < 1 + 1/cross(φ);
  N < 14 for quad2 at the tested scale). Items written at distinct phases contribute crosstalk terms
  with distinct phase factors — the interference sum partially cancels instead of adding coherently.
  Target bar: **MQAR D=256** (the P3-frontier cell the repo deferred) where the param-matched
  Transformer *solves by capacity* — if phase multiplexing pushes the delta state's D-frontier past
  quad2's at matched params, that is a headline the Transformer structurally cannot answer with its
  own O(1)-state-free design, and the D=128 param-efficiency story upgrades to a *capacity-per-param*
  story. Secondary: the same age tag is the principled fix for P1's demodulation risk.
- **HOW (T2, with the derivation sketched for the spec phase).** With k,v,q rotated per token, the
  within-chunk A-matrix entries become k_i·R(j−i)k_j; using R(δ) = cos(ωδ)I + sin(ωδ)J (J = quarter
  turn), each entry is cos(ωδ)(k_i·k_j) + sin(ωδ)(k_i·Jk_j) — **two** inner products per pair instead
  of one; the solve stays an exact unit-lower-triangular solve with modified entries. This mirrors the
  repo's own `eta` precedent (batched per-channel triangular solves, exact to <1e-4
  *(repo-documented)*). Cost: ~2× the A-formation inner products (a fraction of total delta FLOPs),
  solve count unchanged. T1 fallback: chunk-constant rotation, exact via conjugation, zero kernel
  work — run it as the cheap arm and the T2 arm as the real test. `step()==forward()` guard: rotations
  are pointwise, so parity holds by construction; the byte-guard extends to `phase: off`.
- **FALSIFIABLE TEST.** Pre-register: (i) MQAR **D=256** at the 130K-param rung: quad2+P3 solve-rate ≥
  1/3 seeds where quad2-alone = 0/3 *(this baseline cell does not exist yet — run it first, per P9)*;
  (ii) the crosstalk probe: measured `key_crosstalk` metric (repo already computes it) must *decrease*
  with phase multiplexing at fixed D — the mechanism's stated cause must show its stated signature;
  (iii) D-frontier extension 16/32/64/128/256 vs the param-matched TF. If D=256 does not move while
  crosstalk does decrease, capacity is bottlenecked elsewhere — report and kill the capacity claim.
- **RISKS.** (i) T2 kernel derivation is real engineering with a real chance of an exactness bug — the
  repo's <1e-4 reference-vs-chunked guard culture is the mitigation, plus T1 fallback exists. (ii)
  Harmonic readout multiplies read FLOPs by the number of phase hypotheses — cap at 2–4 and put it in
  the FLOP ledger. (iii) Interference cancellation is a mean-field argument; superposition with
  *structured* (non-random) keys may correlate phases and re-cohere the crosstalk — the D=256 test is
  the only honest arbiter. (iv) Closest to "RoPE again" criticism — the rebuttal is positional: RoPE
  phases live in the attention *logit* over a growing cache; here they live in O(1) state *dynamics*;
  the paper must make that sentence load-bearing or the novelty dies.
- **IMPACT 8 / FEASIBILITY 5.**

### P4 — Binding by coherence at readout: gate the head-merge by inter-head phase coherence instead of trusting concatenation

**[B→N]** borrowed: binding-by-synchrony (von der Malsburg 1981; Singer & Gray 1995
*(knowledge)*) and communication-through-coherence (Fries 2005/2015; Womelsdorf 2007 *(knowledge)*).
New: synchrony as a **dynamic gate over the readout merge** of a delta-rule mixer (contested claim —
see Risks; adversarial citation carried).

- **WHAT.** Today heads are independent and their outputs are simply concatenated and linearly merged
  by W_o. Give each head a scalar oscillator phase φ_h(t) — computed per token from x_t by a tiny
  phase head (linear → angle; T0, state-independent) or inherited from P1's ω-integrals — and gate the
  merge with pairwise coherence factors: heads whose phases agree are read out coherently (amplified
  composite); heads in anti-phase are suppressed for this token. Composites become *token-dependent
  assemblies* rather than a fixed learned mixture — binding-by-synchrony as an arithmetic operation on
  the output path, one token at a time.
- **WHY (brain).** Gamma-synchrony binds features into one assembly (Singer & Gray 1995
  *(knowledge)*); coherent ensembles gate their targets (Fries; Womelsdorf *(knowledge)*). The
  artificial mapping is honest at the level of *function* (phase agreement routes combination), not of
  *biophysics* — say so in the paper.
- **WHY (beat transformers).** Attention binds via a softmax over a full O(n²) score matrix; a
  coherence-gated merge is an O(H²) per-token binding with *zero* sequence-length dependence — the
  binding cost stays constant as context grows, which is exactly the regime where the Transformer's
  cache grows linearly. Concretely targets: (a) MQAR at 130K params (better head utilization may lift
  the borderline quad2 seed at 0.935); (b) char-LM (multi-head composition is where tiny models lose
  to bigger ones); (c) interpretability as a deliverable — per-token assembly participation is a
  measurable, plottable artifact (repo culture: an honest observability gain).
- **HOW.** `merge: concat | coherence`; coherence g_hh′ = σ(a·cos(φ_h − φ_h′) + b), applied to the
  merged features before W_o (T0 — pointwise, no kernel contact, `step()` parity trivial). Controls
  are mandatory per repo culture: **random-phase control** (same gate magnitudes, shuffled phases —
  must lose) isolates *phase* from *more gating parameters*; **static-learned-phase control** (fixed
  per-head angles) isolates *dynamics* from *extra capacity*.
- **FALSIFIABLE TEST.** Pre-register: MQAR-130K median ≥ +0.01 over concat baseline AND the
  random-phase control fails to reach the coherence arm's median on ≥2 of 3 seeds (causality of the
  phase relationship, guardrail-#5 style). If coherence ≈ random-phase, the mechanism is generic
  gating — report as a null for the *binding* claim (a generic-gate side-finding may still stand,
  labeled honestly).
- **RISKS.** (i) Binding-by-synchrony is the *contested* neuroscience claim (Shadlen & Movshon 1999
  *(knowledge)*) — the paper must cite its own adversarial literature, and the engineering claim must
  not inherit the biology's confidence. (ii) O(H²) with H=8–12 is trivial now but scales with head
  count — note in ledger. (iii) Learned phases may collapse to constant offsets (degenerate to static
  routing) — the static control detects exactly this.
- **IMPACT 7 / FEASIBILITY 8.**

### P5 — Phase observability audit: measure the oscillations Prizma does not have, before adding any (the control-first proposal)

**[N]** new, and the repo's pre-registration culture applied to this whole axis.

- **WHAT.** Before P1–P4 touch the model, add a *phase ledger* alongside the FLOP ledger: (i) per-head
  effective frequency response of S (probe: impulse-train inputs, readout autocorrelation → per-head
  spectra); (ii) write-gate phase histograms (β_t against within-chunk position — today's β is
  input-dependent, so *something* phase-like may already exist implicitly); (iii) inter-head coherence
  spectra of outputs; (iv) for Prizma-CL, the timing distribution of commit/freeze events relative to
  stream statistics (the CL thread already logs routing — extend to *when*, not just *where*).
  Pre-registered **null: today's Prizma has no phase structure by construction** (real α in [0.5,1]
  cannot oscillate); the audit's job is to make that null *measured*, and to give every later proposal
  a forced comparison: any claimed rhythm must beat this measured baseline, and P9-style "phantom
  oscillation" detections (mechanisms added but unused by the trained model) become detectable.
- **WHY (brain).** None — this is methodology. Its ancestry is the committee's own falsifiability
  discipline.
- **WHY (beat transformers).** Indirectly: it converts the phase axis from vibes into a ledger column
  ("models compared at matched params, FLOPs, *and measured timescale spectra*") — a review-proof
  third axis for the architecture paper, and a cheap way to catch reviewers' "is the rhythm doing
  anything?" before they ask.
- **HOW.** CPU-runnable probes on the existing tiny models; zero changes to training paths; results
  JSON committed like all others.
- **FALSIFIABLE TEST (of the audit itself).** It must *fail* on a synthetic positive: inject a known
  rotation into a test model and confirm the spectra recover the injected frequency — an instrument
  that cannot detect a real oscillation is worse than none.
- **RISKS.** Opportunity cost — it delays the fun mechanisms by days; justified because P1/P3's
  falsifiable tests (spectral signatures) are unreadable without it.
- **IMPACT 5 / FEASIBILITY 9.** *Do first regardless of rank.*

### P6 — Chunk-local phase precession for position: a periodic, sawtoothed phase code replacing learned absolute positions (the cheap length-extrapolation lever)

**[B→N]** borrowed: theta phase precession (position→phase; O'Keefe & Recce 1993 *(knowledge)*);
RoPE's rotation arithmetic (Su et al. 2021 *(knowledge)*). New: *periodic, boundary-resetting* phase
position code tuned to the chunk structure of a recurrent-state model (attention has no boundary to
reset at; Prizma does).

- **WHAT.** Rotate q/k by ω·(t mod C) for a small bank of frequencies — a RoPE that *sawtooths within
  each chunk* instead of growing without bound. Position never exceeds the trained range by
  construction (length extrapolation cannot go out-of-distribution on the position axis); cross-chunk
  order is already carried by the state's causal write order (the repo's stated position source), so
  the phase code only has to supply *within-chunk* fine order — precisely the job the short conv
  currently does implicitly, made explicit and multiplexed.
- **WHY (brain).** Theta precession compresses a short trajectory into phase within one cycle
  (O'Keefe & Recce; Jensen & Lisman *(knowledge)*) — within-chunk position as within-cycle phase is
  the direct analog; the 7±2-items-per-cycle span account (Lisman & Idiart 1995 *(knowledge)*) even
  rhymes with window=16 ⊂ chunk=64.
- **WHY (beat transformers).** Directly targets the length-extrapolation bar (absolute ~0.40 at 8×):
  today the conv is the only fine-position signal and the state read has none; a periodic code is
  extrapolation-safe *by construction* — a claim the RoPE Transformer cannot make (its phase grows
  monotonically; interpolation tricks are repairs of exactly this). Also removes the `learned_pos`
  crutch used for char-LM parity, closing an honest-scope footnote.
- **HOW.** T0 — pointwise rotation before the kernel, reusing the existing (dormant) RoPE code path
  with a modulo on positions and `rope=False` for delta keys preserved; `off == identical` guard
  trivial. Cost: zero parameters, a handful of sin/cos FLOPs — must show up as no-change in the ledger
  (the rare proposal that costs nothing).
- **FALSIFIABLE TEST.** Pre-register on `gpu_lengen.py`: absolute accuracy at 8× ≥ 0.50 *without*
  learned_pos and *without* retraining tricks; at 16× the decay from 8× must be no worse than the
  4×→8× decay (periodicity's signature — a monotone-decay mechanism cannot show it). Control:
  non-modulo (plain RoPE-style) rotation must do *worse* at 8× than the sawtooth — that contrast is
  the test that "periodic" is the active ingredient, not "any rotation."
- **RISKS.** (i) The conv may already supply within-chunk order well enough — the sawtooth must beat
  *conv-only*, not just nothing; run `short_conv=0 + sawtooth` and `short_conv=4 + sawtooth`. (ii)
  Position ambiguity across cycles (t and t+C share phase) is by design delegated to the state — if
  the state is the weak link at long range (it is — that is P1/P3's problem), P6 alone may cap out.
  (iii) Two mechanisms claiming the length-extrapolation win (P1 vs P6) — the report must
  pre-commit to a factorial run and let attribution be measured, not narrated.
- **IMPACT 7 / FEASIBILITY 9.**

### P7 — Consolidation rhythms in Prizma-CL: a global, stream-driven "sleep phase" with freeze hysteresis and PC self-replay (extends report-01's P8 into the CL thread)

**[B→N]** borrowed: two-stage consolidation (Buzsáki 1989; Wilson & McNaughton 1994; Rasch & Born
2013; nested SO–spindle–ripple handoff, Helfrich et al. 2018 *(knowledge)*). New: consolidation
*timing* in a task-boundary-free local learner driven by the model's own surprise rate.

- **WHAT.** Today's freeze is per-expert and *event-driven*: one precision-test failure commits+freezes
  the active expert (`src/prizma.py` — `omega_consol`, single-shot). Make the freeze a *phase* of a
  global oscillator whose speed is the current surprise rate (high surprise → fast clock → frequent
  consolidation windows; quiet stream → long wake epochs): (i) freeze decisions are evaluated over a
  whole window of batches (hysteresis — K-of-N precision failures required, not 1), (ii) during a
  consolidation phase, each committed expert runs a brief *self-replay*: the predictive-coding
  autoencoder generates from its own latent (local, no backprop — the PC decoder run generatively) and
  re-tightens its own precision statistics, (iii) freeze is reversible during the following wake phase
  if the expert's self-recon degrades (soft consolidation, like systems consolidation's graded
  protection; Dudai 2004 *(knowledge)*).
- **WHY (brain).** Encode-then-consolidate on a slow rhythm is the most replication-robust rhythm
  finding in memory neuroscience (Buzsáki two-stage; Rasch & Born 2013 *(knowledge)*); nesting the
  handoff on a slow oscillation (Helfrich 2018 *(knowledge)*) maps onto "consolidation sweeps over all
  experts once per phase."
- **WHY (beat transformers).** Not a Transformer bar — it is the CL thread's bar: premature freezes on
  noisy batches are the documented cause of the graceful-degradation loss as domains overlap
  (`docs/Prizma.md`'s overlap analysis; spurious-consolidation failure mode). Hysteresis + timing
  attacks ACC/FGT in the overlap regime *without boundaries, labels, replay buffers, or backprop* —
  preserving every headline property of the E1 table.
- **HOW.** `src/prizma.py` only: global phase variable, K-of-N freeze rule, optional generative
  self-replay pass (the Expert already has a decoder to generate from — no new learner needed). All
  local; the no-backprop claim is untouched (self-replay is forward-pass generation + the existing
  local rules).
- **FALSIFIABLE TEST.** Pre-register on the existing E-suite: on the overlap-degradation sweep, ACC at
  overlap 0.5–0.8 improves by **≥ +0.03** over current Prizma with FGT still 0.000, AND the measured
  premature-freeze rate drops by ≥50%; the *spurious-freeze probe* (inject single-batch outliers into a
  stable domain) must show the hysteresis arm surviving K−1 interruptions where the current 1-shot arm
  freezes. Self-replay arm: no ACC regression with a bounded compute budget (report the wall-clock
  honestly; replay that wins ACC by costing 10× is a loss per repo norms).
- **RISKS.** (i) "Oscillator" here is a schedule — if phase speed is just inverse batch noise, call it
  adaptive hysteresis and drop the neuroscience branding; the *stream-driven* coupling (surprise-rate
  clock) is the part that earns the analogy and must be ablated against wall-clock/batch-count
  schedules. (ii) Self-replay risks self-reinforcement drift (the model re-teaching itself its errors)
  — bound replay steps, log recon-drift per replay. (iii) Overlap with report-01's P8 — that proposal
  concerns the *seq* thread's encode/retrieve phases; this one is the CL thread's consolidation phase;
  if the owner wants one rhythm story, these are the two ends (fast: P7-seq = encode/retrieve; slow:
  this = sleep) of the same hierarchy.
- **IMPACT 6 / FEASIBILITY 7.**

### P8 — Theta-phase write budget: chunk-level encode/retrieve alternation as a FLOP lever (extends report-01's P8; the phase mechanism that pays rent in the FLOP ledger)

**[B→N]** borrowed: alpha pulsed-inhibition gating (Jensen & Mazaheri 2010 *(knowledge)*); theta
cycle's encode/retrieve segmentation ( Encode/retrieve alternation within a theta cycle — e.g., the
neurally observed phase segregation of incoming vs retrieved content *(knowledge)*). New: applied as a
*chunk-granular compute schedule* on a delta-rule mixer. Overlaps report-01 P8; this section adds what
that report lacked: the kernel-level feasibility ruling and the honest null.

- **WHAT.** Alternate chunk "phases": encode chunks run the delta path at full β; retrieve chunks run
  β→0 for the delta write (kernel still decays the state — decay is free) and give the *window head*
  the full budget (or vice versa), halving per-chunk mixer work in whichever path is idle. Because the
  alternation is chunk-constant, it is T1-exact: no kernel change; the A-matrix on encode chunks is
  untouched, retrieve chunks skip the solve entirely (their delta contribution is pure decay).
- **WHY (brain).** Pulsed inhibition makes alpha a *temporal gate* — computation is routed to the
  permissive phase (Jensen & Mazaheri 2010 *(knowledge)*).
- **WHY (beat transformers).** The repo's biggest honest weakness is the **2.14× forward-FLOP deficit**
  (as-coded vs param-matched TF) and ~5× slower training steps. This is the only proposal whose
  primary effect is *removing* work: a 2-phase schedule can cut mixer FLOPs up to ~2× if read/write
  loads really are dissociable (they may not be — see the test).
- **HOW.** `phase_budget: off | alternate | learned` (learned = a T1 gate predicting per-chunk which
  phase a chunk needs — state-independent, chunk-pooled input statistic). Off == identical guard;
  FLOP ledger re-run per config; latency ledger because training throughput is the binding constraint.
- **FALSIFIABLE TEST.** Pre-register: FLOPs/token ≤ 1.4× TF (from 2.14×) with all §4 diagnostic legs
  non-inferior. The **null arm is the real experiment**: if MQAR (write-heavy) and induction
  (read-heavy) both degrade under alternation but both survive under `learned`, the finding is
  "compute can be reallocated but not removed" — publishable as a negative result on the FLOP lever.
  If even `learned` degrades the diagnostics, the encode/retrieve dissociation does not exist in this
  architecture and the lever dies — that is a clean kill, by design.
- **RISKS.** (i) Highest reinvention risk in this report — "skipping half the writes" is a sparsity
  schedule; the neuroscience name must be earned by the *learned* arm showing chunk-phase selectivity
  that tracks content, not just a 50/50 alternation. (ii) Halving write frequency is functionally
  similar to P2's θ-heads — run the factorial (P2 × P8) once or pre-declare them competitors for the
  same FLOP-rent slot. (iii) Training throughput is kernel-bound, not FLOP-bound (the 5× slowdown is
  sequential-step overhead) — FLOP savings may not show up in wall-clock at all; the ledger must
  measure both and report the gap honestly.
- **IMPACT 6 / FEASIBILITY 6.**

### P9 — Vector-symbol phase binding for expert composition in Prizma-CL (the overlap-regime bet)

**[B→N]** borrowed: holographic/binding superpositions (Plate HRR; Kanerva 2009 HDC; Frady & Sommer
*(knowledge)*); gamma-phase-coded superposition in working memory (Lisman & Jensen 2013
*(knowledge)*). New: binding-composition as the routing object of a vigilance-based MoE.

- **WHAT.** Replace argmin-over-experts routing with a *bound composite*: an input's routing signature
  is a phase-bound superposition of expert signatures (small vectors bound by rotation/phase
  multiplication), so a novel input that is a *mixture* of known domains routes to a coherent
  composite (shared components reinforced, idiosyncratic ones cancelled) instead of collapsing to the
  single best-matching expert. In the overlap regime — where the repo honestly documents graceful
  degradation — this offers compositionality instead of compromise.
- **WHY (brain).** Working memory holds multiple items as phase-coded superpositions without
  catastrophic blending (Lisman & Jensen 2013 *(knowledge)*); binding formalisms make this
  computational (HDC/HRR *(knowledge)*).
- **WHY (beat transformers).** MoE-Transformers (and Prizma-CL) route discretely; a compositional
  routing layer is a differentiator on mixture inputs — but note honestly: **it does not touch the
  proven-impossible regime.** `docs/Prizma.md` proves no single-head learner can solve same-x-
  different-label ambiguity; composite routing does not refute that theorem and must not be sold as if
  it did — its claim is confined to the *partial-overlap* zone of the existing degradation curve.
- **HOW.** Route by nearest composite; train the *matched* expert(s) only (local rule untouched);
  composite inference is a forward-weighted read — no new learning rule. Fully offline, CPU-cheap.
- **FALSIFIABLE TEST.** Pre-register on the existing overlap sweep: ACC at overlap 0.6–0.8 ≥ current
  Prizma **+0.02** with E1 (structured-permuted) non-inferior and the routing ablation (composite vs
  argmin) as the causal arm. If composites only match argmin, the phase-binding formalism is
  unnecessary machinery — kill it and keep argmin.
- **RISKS.** (i) Weakest brain-grounding of the set (binding formalisms are engineering-first);
  (ii) soft routing can contaminate frozen experts through the composite read path — the read must
  stay expert-local with composites only *selecting*, or the FGT=0 quasi-tautology (`docs/Prizma.md`
  §8's own warning) is endangered; (iii) scope creep in the CL thread while the seq thread is the
  strategic front — deprioritize accordingly.
- **IMPACT 5 / FEASIBILITY 4.**

## 3. Cross-cutting honest limits (binding on every proposal above)

1. **Exogeneity problem.** Real rhythms are endogenous; our clocks are token/chunk indices or
   input-conditioned gates. Any brain-claim must survive the "it's a schedule" challenge; the
   data-dependent arms are the defensible versions, and the *learned vs fixed* contrast belongs in
   every paper.
2. **Decoration risk.** The repo has seen levers that ablate to nothing (surprise-gate point
   estimates ran *against* the mechanism; ablations INCONCLUSIVE). Every phase proposal here has a
   pre-registered null and a designated clean-kill condition precisely because this axis is the most
   decoration-prone lens on the committee.
3. **Contested claims carried.** Binding-by-synchrony has standing adversarial literature (Shadlen &
   Movshon 1999 *(knowledge)*); CTC's causal role is debated. Engineering papers may cite the
   controversy; brain-likeness claims must be scoped to *functional* analogies.
4. **Kernel discipline.** Nothing above may ship outside tiers T0–T2. Any proposal that drifts into
   state-dependent gating (T3) inherits the disclosed ~5× sequential-scan cost and must be labeled a
   slow diagnostic arm, never the headline.
5. **Statistical power.** Per the B4 lesson: every headline cell ≥3 seeds, ≥5 for the closest-margin
   leg; the recall-gate contamination incident argues for config-fingerprinted resume caches on all
   new runners before the first phase-mechanism campaign.
