### 3.1 Honest reading

1. **The E1 headline survives the citation-bar audit — and is strengthened.** The boundary-free
   regularizers that were never run do NOT close the gap: best boundary-free ACC is
   OnlineEWC(online) 0.429 ± 0.020 (MAS 0.415 ± 0.019) vs Prizma(DFA) 0.834 ± 0.015 — CIs disjoint
   by ~0.39. Even the strongest new row of any kind (OnlineEWC(boundary), 0.461 ± 0.020, boundary
   in hand) is 0.37 ACC below Prizma and 0.28 below plain replay. "Prizma beats EWC" generalizes
   to "Prizma beats the entire regularizer family, boundary-free included", multi-epoch.
2. **Read FGT next to LA.** SI's spectacular FGT 0.072 comes with LA 0.466: the min-FGT λ rule
   picked λ=100, which protects weights by under-learning them. That is the metric pair working
   as intended, and why the LA column now exists in the tables.
3. **Single-pass (EXPLORATORY): Prizma loses its dominance; replay numerically leads.** Everything
   compresses into the 0.41–0.55 band; replay 0.549 ± 0.019 vs Prizma 0.478 ± 0.042 (borderline
   disjoint CIs). Prizma's FGT (0.185 ± 0.053) is statistically indistinguishable from replay's
   (0.199 ± 0.015) — the deficit is **acquisition** (LA 0.627 vs 0.708): each expert sees 1/15th
   of its E1 gradient budget. This is exactly report 10-P4's prediction ("single-pass is where it
   may crack… finding that is a result, not a failure"). No conclusion is drawn here; §6 exists to
   test it properly.
4. **Tuning-rule honesty.** The min-FGT λ rule (E1's convention) can pick an under-learning point:
   MAS single-pass got λ=100 (seed-0 ACC 0.237) over λ=10 (ACC 0.33) — that row is an upper bound
   on MAS, not a tuned optimum. Every grid value's seed-0 ACC/FGT/validity is in the raw JSON
   (`meta.tuned[arm].trace`); NaN grid-top runs were excluded from selection and flagged.
5. **Prizma protocol verification (as required):** `Prizma.fit_task` = `epochs × permutation ×
   train_batch`; `train_batch` is a per-batch online update (routing/commit/freeze decisions are
   batch-local, no cross-visit state). "Updates are online" is TRUE, but E1 shows each sample 15×.
   The single-pass row changes ONLY the exposure (epochs=1); nothing in the shipped protocol was
   modified. Prizma does implicitly benefit from epochs through extra gradient steps per expert.

## 4. Positioning vs the 2024–26 exemplar-free / task-free SOTA (what reviewers will name)

What the field cites for (near-)exemplar-free and task-free CL, with verification status of this
run: **RanPAC** (McDonnell et al., NeurIPS 2023 — frozen pretrained backbone + random projections +
closed-form prototype heads; verified: NeurIPS 2023 poster page, arXiv:2307.02251).
**SHE** (Wu et al., ICCV 2021 — *as named in commission report 10; the exact acronym expansion was
NOT re-verified this run (search rate limits) — verify before any external use*).
**ACIL** (Zhuang et al., 2022 — "ACIL: Analytic Class-Incremental Learning with Absolute
Memorization and Privacy Protection"; verified on arXiv; the analytic-CL line continued through
AAAI 2024+). Buffer baselines: **GEM** (López-Paz & Ranzato, NeurIPS 2017), **A-GEM** (Chaudhry et
al., ICLR 2019), **ER** (Riemer et al., ICLR 2019), **DER++** (Buzzega et al., NeurIPS 2020),
**MIR** (Aljundi et al., NeurIPS 2019), **GDumb** (Prabhu et al., ECCV 2020). Regularizers:
**EWC** (Kirkpatrick et al., PNAS 2017), **SI** (Zenke et al., ICML 2017), **MAS** (Aljundi et al.,
NeurIPS 2018), **online EWC** (Chaudhry et al., ECCV 2018; Schwarz et al., ICLR-W 2018).
Task-free formulation: **Aljundi et al., "Task-Free Continual Learning", NeurIPS 2019**.
Online single-pass protocols: er-ACE (Caccia et al., 2021, arXiv) and successors.

**Architecture comparability, stated plainly:**

- **RanPAC / SHE / ACIL are NOT architecture-comparable to Prizma** and their numbers must never
  be tabled next to Prizma's. They are pretrained-backbone methods (ViT/ResNet frozen features,
  orders of magnitude larger, evaluated on CIFAR/ImageNet-style class-incremental benchmarks
  Prizma does not touch). The honest relationship is *philosophical adjacency*: frozen random
  projections + local/closed-form heads is the same substrate family as Prizma's fixed-FA + local
  delta heads — report 10 says it first: Prizma's delta versus that family is the **vigilance
  recruitment + consolidation machinery**, not raw ACC on their benchmarks. Until Prizma runs
  their benchmarks (GPU work, out of scope here), zero comparative sentences are permitted.
- **GEM / A-GEM / ER / DER++ / MIR / GDumb are buffer methods** — comparable in spirit (small
  from-scratch nets) but they store data; Prizma does not. Any "beats X" sentence against them
  must state the trade: replay's buffer bytes vs Prizma's per-expert weight growth (which is
  UNBOUNDED in the number of domains — report 10-P3's open hole; "no buffer" is true, "no memory
  growth" is not). The shipped E1 replay row (buffer 1000) already follows this convention.
- **EWC / SI / MAS / online-EWC are directly comparable — and now actually run** (Tables above).
- **Task-free terminology discipline:** the literature's "task-free" (Aljundi 2019) requires no
  boundaries AND no task-count knowledge AND single-pass exposure. Prizma is boundary-free and
  label-free only; its expert pool is sized with the domain count known a priori. Every future
  sentence must say "boundary-free, label-free", never bare "task-free".

**Standing claim boundary (unchanged):** Prizma's CL claims remain restricted to
domain-incremental, input-distinguishable, contiguous-block streams at toy scale
(`docs/Prizma.md` §8). Nothing here creates a class-incremental, pretrained-backbone, or
single-pass claim.

**Claim under test.** In the single-pass domain-incremental protocol (each sample seen exactly
once; configuration frozen below), Prizma is *competitive* with the best boundary-free
regularization baseline — i.e. its no-buffer/no-boundary design does not cost accuracy in the
regime online-CL reviewers actually probe.

**The bar (primary, falsifiable).** Let ACC_L be final average accuracy (mean over seeds) of
learner L, and let B* be the boundary-free regularizer arm (MAS, OnlineEWC(online)) with the
highest seed-0 ACC under the frozen tuning rule (decided before the run, from seed 0 only).
**PASS iff** ACC_PRIZMA − max(ACC_backprop, ACC_B*) ≥ −0.05 AND the two-sided Welch 95% CI on the
seed-level difference (ACC_PRIZMA − ACC_B*) excludes values below −0.05. Secondary (reported, not
gated): FGT_PRIZMA ≤ FGT_B* + 0.05; Prizma vs replay (buffer arm) reported descriptively, never
claimed as a win.

**Arms.** Exactly the Table-2 battery: backprop, EWC, MAS, SI, OnlineEWC(online),
OnlineEWC(boundary), replay, PRIZMA(DFA) — same substrate, stream, metrics, and per-protocol
seed-0 tuning rule as `experiments/run_citation_battery.py` at the registered commit.

**Seeds (fresh, stopping rule fixed).** Seeds **10–19** (n=10), contiguous, fixed in advance, no
peeking, no additions ("add seeds until significant" is forbidden by POLICY.md). The exploration
used 0–9 precisely so this run's seeds are uncontaminated.

**Statistics.** Welch's t 95% CI on the paired-by-stream seed-level ACC differences; margin
δ = 0.05; α = 0.05; ONE primary comparison (vs B*) — no multiplicity correction needed beyond that
per POLICY.md §4; all other contrasts labeled secondary/exploratory in the verdict text.

**Power note (honest).** Exploratory seed-level ACC SDs from Table 2 raw records: Prizma 0.068,
OnlineEWC(online) 0.032, MAS 0.065. The Welch 95% CI half-width for the primary contrast at n=10
per arm is therefore ≈ 0.05–0.07 — the bar's δ = 0.05 is at the edge of resolvability. If the CIs
straddle −0.05, the verdict word is **INCONCLUSIVE**, not PASS; a follow-up would need n ≈ 15–20
seeds (addendum territory, decided by the maintainer).
